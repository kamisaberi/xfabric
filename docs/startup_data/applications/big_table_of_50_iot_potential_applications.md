### Why xFabric Wins the IoT Market

1.  **The "Fragmented Chip" Problem:**
    IoT devices use the cheapest chips available (RISC-V, ARM, MIPS).
    *   *Competitors:* Struggle to support these weird chips.
    *   *xFabric:* **xInfer** abstracts this. You can run the "NannyCam" app on a $10 Rockchip cam or a $50 Raspberry Pi seamlessly.

2.  **The "Service Revenue" Model:**
    Hardware is a one-time sale. The **xFabric Marketplace** creates recurring revenue.
    *   *Scenario:* A user buys a security camera for $50. They pay **$5/month** to subscribe to the "Advanced Intruder Detection" algorithm from the marketplace.
    *   *Profit:* You take 30% of that subscription forever.

3.  **Security & Updates:**
    IoT is infamous for being insecure (the "S" in IoT stands for Security, as the joke goes).
    *   **xFabric Deployer** solves this. You provide a secure, encrypted, auto-updating OS for these cheap devices, making them enterprise-grade secure.
